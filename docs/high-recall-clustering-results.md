# Simultaneous precision and recall target search

Target: precision >82%, recall >90%, distant-turn recall >80%, simultaneously. None of the calibration-selected candidates below meets all targets on the 52-thread reused development evaluation subset.

A later [supervised affinity candidate](learned-turn-affinity-results.md) clears the three point-estimate targets. The negative results below remain part of the experimental record; the candidate has separate generalization and grouping-quality limitations.

| Sweep | Calibration P | Calibration R | Calibration distant R | Evaluation P | Evaluation R | Evaluation distant R | Evaluation F1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Code 4 2048 BIRCH, 256 trials | 81.47% | 92.42% | 82.14% | 77.83% | 89.12% | 77.78% | 83.09% |
| Convex BIRCH ensemble, 512 trials | 83.52% | 90.46% | 86.90% | 76.60% | 93.26% | 88.89% | 84.11% |
| Alternative partition families, 768 trials | 83.11% | 90.22% | 88.10% | 76.72% | 92.23% | 88.89% | 83.76% |
| Complete-link group refinement, 33 trials | 83.52% | 90.46% | 86.90% | 76.60% | 93.26% | 88.89% | 84.11% |

The first selected setting uses Code 4, radius .45, membership cutoff .675, relative margin .20 and union views. The ensemble uses .25 Code/.75 Voyage, BIRCH radius .45, membership cutoff .6375, relative margin .20 and chunk view. The family sweep selects complete-link, partition cosine .60, the same ensemble weight, membership cutoff/margin and chunk view. Its `radius` report field denotes partition cosine, not BIRCH radius.

The frozen Voyage 4 reference remains 83.66% precision, 87.56% recall and 70.37% distant recall. The original high-recall Code 4 2048 candidate remains 75.21% precision, 92.75% recall and 81.48% distant recall. The new ensemble reaches 48/54 distant related pairs, compared with 38/54 for the reference, but has 55 false positives versus 33. Overall evaluation counts for the three sweeps are respectively TP/FP/FN/TN = 172/49/21/54, 180/55/13/48 and 178/54/15/49. Each excludes 50 ambiguous judgments. Calibration has 409 related, 188 unrelated and 68 ambiguous judgments, with 84 distant related pairs. Evaluation has 193 related and 103 unrelated judgments. Thus distant-recall estimates have coarse resolution and do not imply corpus-wide rates.

Two calibration candidates pass the targets but evaluation precision drops about seven percentage points. This is evidence that the calibration success does not generalize here; it is not evidence that additional radius tuning will fix the problem. The ensemble's exploratory 95% paired thread-bootstrap F1-gain interval versus the reference is [-3.86,+1.11] percentage points; complete-link's is [-5.27,+1.78]. Neither establishes a balanced-quality improvement. A [33-trial weak-link refinement](high-recall-refinement-protocol.md) splits each overlapping centroid group by complete-link on unsmoothed joint-turn vectors. Floors .05–.35 do not change the winning ensemble's calibration outcomes; stronger floors lose recall. Selection retains the disabled-refinement ensemble. Thus the duplicated evaluation row is an exact control replay, not another distinct quality win. The scored-pair inclusion rules remain unchanged.

Procedures were registered before each run: [settings](high-recall-clustering-protocol.md), [ensemble](high-recall-ensemble-protocol.md), [partition families](high-recall-families-protocol.md). Selection sees only 104 calibration threads; one frozen candidate per sweep is scored on the separate 52-thread development subdivision. That subdivision was already consumed in earlier research and later sweeps are adaptive follow-ups. Labels are blind agent-authored and provisional. Confirmation remains sealed; no baseline or production model is changed.

All sweeps use cached joint-turn embeddings, preserve input ownership and branch-aware smoothing, and cost $0 in additional inference. Optuna records all 1,569 trials; MLflow stores only aggregate metrics and provenance. Raw matrices, labels and predictions remain private. Driver elapsed times excluding final Optuna SQLite persistence are 20.6s, 52.1s, 62.1s and 21.9s; these are experimental scoring times, not embedding or ingestion benchmarks. Ruff/ty and all 168 Python tests pass. Executed sources are archived separately from later style-only changes, and hashes/trials/MLflow states are verified. No production model or frozen baseline changes. The next research question is the nature of remaining false positives, rather than an unregistered extension of this search.
