## The Multi-Agent Search History Tool

Find things you've already worked out with Claude Code, Codex, GitHub Copilot,
Cursor, OpenCode, and Antigravity.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Pick up where you left off" icon="open-book">
    Find an earlier decision and read the messages behind it. Give your next session a useful starting point.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bring your chats together" icon="magnifier">
    Search across supported agents from your terminal, inside your agent, or in the local dashboard.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Keep your history local" icon="laptop">
    Index and search on your machine. Sharing across your own machines is optional.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Follow the evidence" icon="document">
    Open the original messages around a search result and see what was actually discussed.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Start with a question

Ask your agent: **“What did we decide about authentication last week?”**

Or search directly:

```sh
mash search 'authentication'
```

New to Mash? The [quick start](/quick-start/) covers installation, the local search service, and your first search.
