the immutable filtered-hashed fixed-TF and32-block-calibrated rotation trees,
both arrival orders, unchanged. Their failed uncut hierarchy gates remain

Reuse already captured pooled raw MiniLM block directions as independent
semantic geometry; no new inference, model/LLM/download/held-out read. For each
meeting compute all distinct physical-block cosine mean and population SD.
Cut threshold=mean+one SD, a heuristic rather than a calibrated hypothesis test.
Top-down accept a node with at least2 blocks only if its mean distinct-pair
semantic cosine exceeds that threshold by more than the existing1e-12 numerical
margin (frozen before outcomes, not a tuned semantic threshold). Otherwise descend. Leaves
that cannot join a qualifying group remain explicitly unassigned. Duplicate-only
geometry has mean1/SD0 and cannot gain a fictitious topic through equality.
Average node cosine uses the exact pair-mean identity
(squared norm of leaf sum minus sum of squared leaf norms)/(n*(n-1)); no
renormalized-centroid surrogate or cubic nested pair scan. Record null statistics,
every node score and selected cut. Validate against direct pairs on fixtures.

Factorial controls: fixed-eight largest-population display vs semantic cut,
each with existing static-noise c-TF-IDF labels vs source-supported distinctive
labels. No gold topic count/cut, source-topic names or query text as features.
Distinctive candidate terms must contain a letter, be outside the unchanged
static stoplist, occur in at least2 eligible member blocks and <=40percent of
all8196 physical corpus blocks. These frequency settings reuse the earlier
topic-label vocabulary controls; no new list of individually bad filler words.
Score existing c-TF-IDF times smoothed corpus block IDF; top8 positive terms,
lexical ties. Existing raw-fallback blocks contribute no invented label evidence.
Source-word DF is computed from the available initial corpus snapshot for batch
label diagnostics, not an online causal DF claim or changed geometry weights.

All proposed cut groups retained in the report. Groups with fewer than2 label
terms are flagged weak and not promoted; sources in them remain unassigned for
the usable-topic diagnostic. This is label-support abstention, not noise gold.
Compare both proposal and usable memberships. Preserve all original source
identities and original gold-positive pair denominators when reporting pair
precision/recall/F1, including far (>4 original turns) and recurring
(far plus distinct native spans) scopes. Only singleton-known sources are
conditional negatives; unknown/mixed sources remain in geometry and coverage.
Recurring precision includes far distinct-known-topic pairs as eligible negatives;
same-topic/same-native-span pairs are outside that recurring diagnostic, not
false negatives or false positives. Unassigned sources never become a common
predicted cluster. Undefined precision/recall is explicit null, never1.
Report source/block assignment coverage, fragment sizes and actual labels/
representatives; conditional precision alone is not a quality improvement.
Representatives use the mean of original semantic vectors within the proposed
group, dot products in ascending block order, and the earliest exact argmax.

arithmetic/tie rule is frozen before corpus outcomes; reassociation can affect
nearly tied choices and must be audited separately from topic quality.
